Two-Company Financial Statement Comparison

Enter figures from each company's income statement and balance sheet to compare liquidity, profitability, leverage, and efficiency.

1. Enter the data

Enter all amounts in the same units (such as $ millions) for the same fiscal year. Pull them from each company's 10-K on SEC EDGAR. The sample data is hypothetical.

2. Results

Ratio

Shaded cells mark the stronger company on that measure. Ratios use year-end balances, and the quick ratio is approximated as (cash + receivables) / current liabilities.

3. DuPont breakdown of ROE

Component

ROE = net margin × asset turnover × equity multiplier. It shows whether returns come from profitability, efficiency, or leverage.

Ratios only compare well within the same industry. A grocer and a software company have structurally different margins and balance sheets.
